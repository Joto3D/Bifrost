First time opening it

Both operating systems double-check apps that don't come from an app store. That's normal — here's exactly what to click.

On a Mac

  1. Open the downloaded Bifrost-macOS.zip — it unzips itself into Bifrost.app.

  2. Optional: drag Bifrost.app into your Applications folder.

  3. Right-click Bifrost.app → Open → click Open again in the popup. Do this the very first time instead of double-clicking.

Mac shows a warning here because Bifrost is made by an indie developer, not a big company with an Apple certificate — that's expected, not a sign anything's wrong.
Mac says the app is "damaged" and won't open?

This happens because macOS flags files downloaded from the internet — it's not actually damaged. Open Terminal (search for it with Spotlight) and paste this, then press Return:

xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/Bifrost.app

Then try opening Bifrost again as above. If you moved it to Applications first, use that path instead of ~/Downloads.

On Windows

  1. Open the downloaded Bifrost-win-x64.zip and click Extract all.

  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click Bifrost.exe.

  3. If Windows SmartScreen pops up ("Windows protected your PC"), click More info, then Run anyway.

SmartScreen shows this for any new indie app that hasn't built up a download history yet — it's a reputation check, not a virus warning.

What Bifrost does for you

All the fiddly parts of modding Valheim, handled.

One-click installs

Grab mods straight from Thunderstore or Nexus with a single click — dependencies resolve automatically.

Mod profiles

Keep separate modlists for different worlds or servers, and switch between them in seconds.

Automatic save backups

Bifrost quietly backs up your world saves so a bad mod update can never cost you progress.

Safe multiplayer mode

Play modded with friends without risking a mismatch kicking you out of the server mid-raid.

Six themes

Pick the look that fits your setup, from cozy to cosmic.

No command line, ever

Every step — from finding Valheim to launching modded — happens through a normal app window.
